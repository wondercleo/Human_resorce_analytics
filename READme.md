IBM HR Analytics & Workforce Executive DashboardExecutive OverviewThis repository contains an end-to-end Human Resources Data Analytics project built using Excel and Power Query on the IBM HR Analytics master dataset (4,410 employee records). The project transforms raw operational data and employee survey feedback into actionable insights through robust data cleaning, feature engineering, multi-dimensional PivotTable analyses, and a dynamic, interactive Executive Excel Dashboard.Technical Stack & Tools UsedData Ingestion & ETL: Microsoft Power Query (Left Outer Joins, Type Casting, Null Handling, Column Removal).Feature Engineering: Excel Advanced Logic Formulas (IFS, TRANSPOSE, UNIQUE, COUNTA).Analytical Engine: Excel PivotTables, Custom Calculated Fields (% of Row Total, Value Field Settings).Data Visualization & UX: Interactive Excel Dashboard, 100% Stacked Bar Charts, Clustered Columns, Dynamic Slicers with Report Connections.Data Architecture & Integration Workflow1. Data Sources & Quality AuditThe master data model integrates three distinct relational datasets linked via EmployeeID as the primary key:general_data.csv (Workforce demographics, salary, tenure, department, turnover).employee_survey_data.csv (Environment satisfaction, job satisfaction, work-life balance ratings).manager_survey_data.csv (Job involvement, annual performance ratings).Audit Summary & Key Findings:Record Integrity: 4,410 unique employee records across all tables.Missing Value Treatments: Identified string "NA" values across numerical fields (NumCompaniesWorked: 19 missing, TotalWorkingYears: 9 missing, EnvironmentSatisfaction: 25 missing) and converted them to standard nulls in Power Query.Data Type Rectification: Converted identifier fields to Integer (123), financial/tenure fields to numeric, and categorical attributes to Text (ABC).Zero-Variance Column Pruning: Removed redundant, uninformative columns where variance across all rows was zero (Over18, StandardHours, EmployeeCount).2. Under 5k", MonthlyIncome<=10000, "2. 5k-10k", MonthlyIncome<=15000, "3. 10k-15k", MonthlyIncome>15000, "4. 15k+")Tenure Band: =IFS(YearsAtCompany<=2, "0-2 Years", YearsAtCompany<=5, "3-5 Years", YearsAtCompany<=10, "6-10 Years", YearsAtCompany>10, "10+ Years")Data Dictionary & SchemaField NameData TypeSource TableDescription / NotesEmployeeIDWhole NumberMaster KeyUnique employee identifier (1 Core Visualizations (2x2 Grid Layout)Top-Left (Departmental Turnover): Clustered Bar Chart evaluating attrition rates across organizational departments.Top-Right (Satisfaction vs. Turnover): 100% Stacked Bar Chart illustrating attrition percentage progression across Job Satisfaction tiers (Level 1 Low to Level 4 Very High).Bottom-Left (Workload & Overtime Analysis): Clustered Column Chart measuring the attrition spike among employees working overtime vs standard hours.Bottom-Right (Demographic Risk Profiles): Grouped Column Chart analyzing attrition distribution across Age Bands and Income Bands.Key Insights & HR RecommendationsSatisfaction & Retention Link: Attrition rates jump significantly among staff rating Job Satisfaction as Level 1 (Low) or Level 2 (Medium), particularly in high-pressure roles.Tenure Critical Window: The highest concentration of voluntary turnover occurs within the 0-2 Years tenure band, indicating a need for enhanced onboarding and early-career retention strategies.Compensation & Age Vulnerability: Staff in lower income bands (Under 5k) within younger age demographics (Under 25 and 25-34) exhibit elevated turnover, suggesting entry-level salary benchmarking is required.How to Navigate the WorkbookDashboard Tab: Interactive executive view.
